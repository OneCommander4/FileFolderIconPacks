Detected license family: GNU GPL

Practical checklist for downloadable OneCommander ZIPs:
- Keep the redistributed artwork under the applicable GPL and include the complete license and copyright notices.
- The ZIP contains editable SVG/PNG source-form artwork. If you later distribute only rendered or compiled forms, also provide the complete corresponding source and necessary build/processing materials.
- Mark material changes. Renaming/reorganizing alone should still be disclosed, as done in SOURCE.txt.
- Publishing the entire upstream repository is not automatically required for a source-form subset, but hosting a matching source archive or repository is the simplest conservative compliance option.
- Do not impose additional restrictions that conflict with recipients’ GPL rights.
- Keep AUTHORS.txt, LICENSE.txt, and SOURCE.txt inside every downloadable Files or Folders pack.
- Re-check upstream licensing when refreshing the artwork.

This is a practical compliance summary, not legal advice.
