Detected license family: MIT

Practical checklist for downloadable OneCommander ZIPs:
- Include the upstream copyright notice and MIT permission text with every distributed ZIP.
- The MIT license does not require publishing the full upstream repository or your repackaging scripts.
- Preserve separate notices for any artwork identified as having another license.
- Keep AUTHORS.txt, LICENSE.txt, and SOURCE.txt inside every downloadable Files or Folders pack.
- Re-check upstream licensing when refreshing the artwork.

This is a practical compliance summary, not legal advice.
