Detected license family: GNU LGPL

Practical checklist for downloadable OneCommander ZIPs:
- Include the LGPL text, copyright notices, attribution, and source provenance.
- Keep modifications to LGPL-covered artwork available in preferred editable form.
- Publishing the entire upstream repository is not automatically required for an unmodified source-form subset, but retain a matching source archive and clear upstream link.
- Do not add restrictions that prevent recipients from exercising LGPL rights.
- Keep AUTHORS.txt, LICENSE.txt, and SOURCE.txt inside every downloadable Files or Folders pack.
- Re-check upstream licensing when refreshing the artwork.

This is a practical compliance summary, not legal advice.
